# Dataset Credit

This project uses the **AG News Topic Classification Dataset**.

Dataset CSV repository:
https://github.com/mhjabreel/CharCnn_Keras/tree/master/data/ag_news_csv

Direct files used by the notebook:

- train.csv: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mhjabreel/CharCnn_Keras/master/data/ag_news_csv/train.csv
- test.csv: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mhjabreel/CharCnn_Keras/master/data/ag_news_csv/test.csv

The AG News topic classification benchmark was constructed by Xiang Zhang from the AG corpus and used in:

Zhang, X., Zhao, J., & LeCun, Y. (2015). Character-level Convolutional Networks for Text Classification. NIPS 2015.

Paper:
https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.01626

The notebook downloads the dataset automatically at runtime and does not redistribute the CSV files.
